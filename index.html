Our Story

Two travellers, one long pause.

Digvijay and Vaishnavi met at JNU, where a library correction led to years of long conversations, late chai and a life shaped by travel. After twenty years in the Indian Foreign Service, they moved through more than sixty countries, carrying a growing love for coffee and the rituals that made each place feel like home.

In every city, they began the day the same way: finding the best cup in town. A tiny roastery in Addis Ababa, a quiet bar in Trieste, a rooftop in Medellin, a farmhouse in Coorg at sunrise. What began as a habit became a way of understanding a place.

Now they have returned to Dehradun, where the hills, the cool mornings and the unhurried pace feel like the right place to stop. Coffee and Cuddles is their small corner of the world: warm, thoughtful and open to slow, generous mornings and evenings.
